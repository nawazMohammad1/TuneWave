TUNEWAVE PRIVACY

Your privacy. Your trust.

We believe your music experience should be personal, transparent, and secure. This Privacy Policy explains how TuneWave collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last Updated: September 18, 2026

01

Introduction

Welcome to TuneWave. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you provide while using our website and services.

This Privacy Policy describes what information we may collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

02

Information We Collect

When you use TuneWave, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide to us.

Account Information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Username
  • Password
  • Profile information

Usage Information

  • Songs and playlists you interact with
  • Artists and albums you explore
  • Recently played content
  • Search activity
  • General website interaction
03

How We Use Your Information

Information collected by TuneWave may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain our services.
  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Personalize your music experience.
  • Improve website performance and functionality.
  • Respond to questions and support requests.
  • Protect our platform against misuse and unauthorized activity.
04

Cookies

TuneWave may use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences, understand website usage, and improve your browsing experience.

Cookies may help us understand how visitors interact with different areas of the website.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

05

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with TuneWave against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of storing or transmitting information over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

06

Sharing of Information

TuneWave does not share personal information with third parties except when reasonably necessary to operate, maintain, secure, or improve our services, or when required by applicable law.

We do not sell your personal information as part of the normal operation of the TuneWave service.

07

Third-Party Services

TuneWave may use third-party services to support certain website functions, such as analytics, authentication, hosting, or other technical services.

These third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

08

Children's Privacy

TuneWave is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us without appropriate permission, please contact us so that the information can be reviewed.

09

Your Privacy Choices

Depending on the features available on TuneWave, you may be able to access, update, or delete certain information associated with your account.

You may also contact us regarding questions about your personal information or privacy preferences.

10

Changes to This Policy

TuneWave may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, technology, or legal requirements.

When changes are made, the updated version will be posted on this page together with the revised update date.

11

Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, you can contact TuneWave.

Email privacy@tunewave.com
