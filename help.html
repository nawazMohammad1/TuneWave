FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Quick answers.

Here are answers to some common TuneWave questions.

01

How do I create a TuneWave account?

Select the Sign Up option and provide the requested information. Once your account is created, you can manage your profile and explore TuneWave.

02

How can I find music?

Use the Search feature or visit the Explore page to discover songs, artists, albums, playlists, and genres.

03

How do I discover playlists?

Visit the Playlists page to browse playlists designed for different moods, activities, and moments.

04

Why can't I find a particular song?

Try checking the spelling of the song, artist, or album name. You can also browse music through genres and curated sections.

05

What should I do if TuneWave isn't working properly?

Try refreshing the page, checking your internet connection, or reopening your browser. If the issue continues, contact our support team.

06

How can I learn about TuneWave's privacy practices?

Visit our Privacy Policy page for information about the information TuneWave may collect and how it is used.
